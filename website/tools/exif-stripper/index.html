Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about photo metadata, GPS tracking, and Pexoris ExifStripper.

What metadata is stored inside my photos? +
Smartphones and cameras store EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) data including your precise GPS latitude and longitude, camera brand, model, lens serial number, date and time down to the second, exposure settings, and software version.
Does Pexoris ExifStripper degrade photo quality? +
No. The visual image resolution, color depth, and sharpness remain visually lossless. Pexoris only removes the header metadata segments and saves using maximum quality encoding.
Why not use online web photo metadata removers? +
Online web removers require you to upload your personal, potentially sensitive photos to third-party cloud servers. You have no guarantee of who accesses or caches those images. Pexoris ExifStripper executes 100% locally on your computer with zero internet connectivity.
What happens to vertical or rotated portrait photos? +
Many metadata strippers wipe the EXIF orientation tag, which causes portrait photos to display sideways or upside down. Pexoris ExifStripper inspects the orientation tag first, physically corrects the image rotation, and then safely sanitizes the headers.
