Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my Bluetooth toggle disappear from Windows 11 Quick Settings? +
The Bluetooth toggle disappears when the underlying Bluetooth Support Service (bthserv) crashes or gets set to Disabled, or when Windows enables aggressive Selective Suspend power saving on the USB radio chip. When the controller enters a low-power state and fails to wake, Windows hides the toggle entirely. Pexoris BluetoothFixer forces bthserv to Automatic, disables selective suspend, and re-enumerates the bus in 1 click.
How does Pexoris BluetoothFixer fix Bluetooth audio stuttering and lag? +
Wireless headphone audio stuttering occurs when the Bluetooth Handsfree Audio Gateway Service (BthHFSrv) conflicts with the A2DP Stereo profile or when the Windows Audio Engine (Audiosrv) buffer falls out of synchronization. Pexoris BluetoothFixer cycles the audio subsystem and re-aligns the endpoint handles without requiring a restart.
What causes Bluetooth Code 43 or Code 10 in Device Manager? +
Code 43 and Code 10 indicate that the Bluetooth radio controller firmware crashed or failed its power state transition handshake with the ACPI bus. Pexoris BluetoothFixer flushes the controller's power policy registry keys and executes an automated PnP hardware re-enumeration to reinitialize the radio.
Will Pexoris BluetoothFixer delete my paired Bluetooth devices? +
No. Pexoris BluetoothFixer repairs the operating system services, protocol drivers, and power management flags. Your existing paired keyboards, mice, headphones, and controllers remain paired and reconnect automatically.
Does Pexoris BluetoothFixer run in the background? +
No. Pexoris strictly adheres to a zero-resident standard. The executable performs the requested Bluetooth stack repairs directly on your operating system and terminates completely with 0% memory or CPU overhead.
