FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS, privacy, and Pexoris DoH Switcher.

What is DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) and why is it essential in Windows 11? ▼
Traditional DNS queries are broadcast in clear text over port 53. Anyone on your local network, your ISP, or government snoops can intercept and record every site you look up. DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) wraps your DNS queries inside TLS encrypted HTTPS connections (port 443), making your DNS requests indistinguishable from normal secure web browsing.
How does Pexoris DoH Switcher block ads system-wide without extensions? ▼
When you select AdGuard DNS, known ad-serving networks, tracking telemetry, and phishing domains are filtered at the DNS resolution stage before your browser even sends a web request. This saves bandwidth, speeds up page loading, and blocks intrusive ads across all browsers and desktop software.
Does this tool replace a VPN? ▼
For website unblocking and privacy from ISP DNS snooping, yes! Unlike VPNs which route all your traffic through foreign servers and throttle your download speeds, DoH Switcher only encrypts your domain lookups while preserving 100% of your native internet bandwidth.
How does Pexoris compare to Sordum DNS Jumper? ▼
Sordum's DNS Jumper is a classic tool from the Windows 7 era that only supports unencrypted port 53 DNS. Pexoris DoH Switcher brings this concept to the modern era with genuine Windows 11 encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) templates, active ping benchmarking, clean modern aesthetics, and open-source architecture.
Can I restore my original router DNS settings? ▼
Yes, at any time. Simply click the "Restore Default (DHCP)" button. The tool immediately instructs Windows to obtain DNS automatically from your router and flushes the local DNS cache.
Is Pexoris DoH Switcher safe and free from malware? ▼
Yes, 100%. Pexoris DoH Switcher is free forever, MIT open-source, contains zero adware, zero bloatware, and zero telemetry. Every release includes its cryptographic SHA-256 hash so you can verify binary integrity.
